**Tried and reverted: `(R-PlaceReturn)`**, which builds the returned literal's vector in the
caller's buffer store.  It was correct everywhere and slower everywhere it reached (`insert_text`
+15 %).  A fresh store grows at its tail, while the shared store relocates the vector and costs
the loop its argument holders.  The rule text and analysis record why, and the cells stay as
guards.  The patch is `target-exp/tools/placereturn.patch`.  Lesson: profile the row's own
share before designing, because the "copy" `insert_text` paid was one 10 KB block.
