## The goal (owner) — reached

**Every re-free is a compiler defect; the interpreter fails the run at one, by default.**
"Re-free" = a free of a store that is already free.  Both runtimes refuse it by slot number
only, so a store allocated in between is released by the second free; the C backend ships what
the compiler emits.

## The rule every fix follows (CLAUDE.md, owner)

A store-lifetime defect is fixed in **three places**: the compiler, the store records
(`src/store_records.rs`), and the checker — with the cure switched off (a `LOFT_NO_*` /
opt-out switch), `scripts/own_sweep.py --oracle check-records --cell <probe> --switch <SW>`
reads CAUGHT.  Each cure so far has its switch documented (STORE_RECORDS.md § The checks, or
BOTH_BACKEND_SWITCHES.md, which sits at 999 lines: move history to its `-history.md` before
adding).  Commit with `Contract: settled — @FR-…` and the two attribution lines.

## Instruments

- The failure message: `a store was freed twice: store #N freed at <file:line:col> (pc=A),
  freed again at <file:line:col> (pc=B)`.  `pc` is the `@abs` scale of `loft introspect`
  (function start = the `@abs` on its `return-address` line).  Stdlib instances are not in
  `introspect`'s IR; `LOFT_LOG=all_fns` prints their IR.
- `LOFT_TRACE_DB=1` prints every free with its `pc=` (interpreter frees carry no name).
- `LOFT_DOUBLE_FREE=1`: census instead of failure, both backends (C: `-DLOFT_DOUBLE_FREE`, prints
  different-site pairs with fn and line).  `own_sweep.py` arms it and counts `re-free` as a
  runtime fault.
- `LOFT_OWN_ORACLE=check-records`: the records name the variable re-freed
  (`store-re-free [...] \`x\` is freed by OpFreeRef after its store was already freed`).
- Run probes and sweeps under `ulimit -v 8000000`, `LOFT_NO_CACHE=1`, on a frozen copy of the
  binary (`cp target/release/loft $S/binN/loft; ln -s $PWD/default $S/binN/default`).
