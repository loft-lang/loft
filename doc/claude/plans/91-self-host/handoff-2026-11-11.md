## The goal in flight (owner)

**Every re-free is a compiler defect; the interpreter fails the run at one, by default.**
"Re-free" = a free of a store that is already free.  Both runtimes refuse it by slot number
only, so a store allocated in between is released by the second free; the C backend ships what
the compiler emits (owner, 2026-11-11: "prevent re-frees where possible", then "make this the
default, let the interpreter fail ... and repair the remaining cases").

## The rule every fix follows (CLAUDE.md, owner)

A store-lifetime defect is fixed in **three places**: the compiler, the store records
(`src/store_records.rs`), and the checker — with the cure switched off (a `LOFT_NO_*` /
opt-out switch), `scripts/own_sweep.py --oracle check-records --cell <probe> --switch <SW>`
reads CAUGHT.  Each cure so far has its switch documented (STORE_RECORDS.md § The checks, or
BOTH_BACKEND_SWITCHES.md, which sits at 999 lines: move history to its `-history.md` before
adding).  Commit with `Contract: settled — @FR-…` and the two attribution lines.

## Instruments

- The failure message: `a store was freed twice: store #N freed at <file:line:col> (pc=A),
  freed again at <file:line:col> (pc=B)`.  `pc` is the `@abs` scale of `loft introspect`
  (function start = the `@abs` on its `return-address` line).  Stdlib instances are not in
  `introspect`'s IR; `LOFT_LOG=all_fns` prints their IR.
- `LOFT_TRACE_DB=1` prints every free with its `pc=` (interpreter frees carry no name).
- `LOFT_DOUBLE_FREE=1`: census instead of failure, both backends (C: `-DLOFT_DOUBLE_FREE`, prints
  different-site pairs with fn and line).  `own_sweep.py` arms it and counts `re-free` as a
  runtime fault.
- `LOFT_OWN_ORACLE=check-records`: the records name the variable re-freed
  (`store-re-free [...] \`x\` is freed by OpFreeRef after its store was already freed`).
- Run probes and sweeps under `ulimit -v 8000000`, `LOFT_NO_CACHE=1`, on a frozen copy of the
  binary (`cp target/release/loft $S/binN/loft; ln -s $PWD/default $S/binN/default`).

## The remaining 131, by cause

The common shape: a binding that only VIEWS a store is typed as owning it (empty dep list,
`@FR-O-Proxy`), so it gets a free of its own beside the real owner's.  The settling rules are
`@FR-B-View` (a projection borrows its base), `@FR-O-Buffer` (a buffer is witnessed by the
local that adopts it), `@FR-O-Complete` (one owner per store, per path).

1. **Closures — 78 cells (loft3-c1's).**  A fn-ref local and the `___clos_N` record it holds both
   free the record; captured vectors freed by the record's cascade and by the frame.  loft3-c1
   has three cures on **their** branch `tuxedo-pr2448-next`: `e2d195609` (fn-ref stands down for
   a local closure, `LOFT_NO_FNREF_STAND_DOWN`), `667b9cbbb` (`captured_record_stand_down`,
   `captured_view_stand_down`).  Not yet on `91-self-host` — get them joined (loft3 joins) or
   ask loft3-c1 for patches against this head, then re-measure.
2. **A field / element / tuple member read off a call's result — ~22 cells, records miss them.**
   `x = fresh_h(k).e` (`parser/nullable_slots.rs` `enum_slot_read_named`), `e = mk().items`
   (`__lift_1 = n_mk(__ref_1); e = OpGetField(__lift_1, …)`, `e` without a dep on `__lift_1`),
   `pa = mkv(a)[0]` (`parser/fields.rs:3757` `inline_container`: `__ref_p2_1` freed beside
   `__ref_1`, one store), `__tret_1.0` (`parser/control.rs:5237` `synthetic_tuple_return`).
   Cells: L-Null-Which/2443, O-Move/1101 (`lift_bind`, `lift_return`), F-Ret/2443, C-Tuple
   heap-tuple, B-View/2362 (`t: Ty?` holding unit-enum literals — `@FR-O-Witness`).  The records

3. **Slices / vector projections — ~10 cells.**  `a = v[..-1] + [9]` (the slice block typed
