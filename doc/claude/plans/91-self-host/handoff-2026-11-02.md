  including field destinations (`S { v: a + [6] }`, `g.v = [1] + [2]`); a slice in a concat is a

  helpers.

  `v[x#index]` in `for x in v`, and `(N-Guard)` guards on `&&` / `||` and `||` guard clauses.
  `v[-1]` is preferred over `v[len(v) - 1]`: equal under the compiler, faster interpreted.

  the position in the vector `(I-Index)`; **#2344** / **#2345** a comprehension over a slice or
