  problem — most of rustc will be shelved; the C writer's clauses are what is judged.
- **c4-view S2 closed** (c4-view-plan.md § S2): the forms of `(R-ValueRecord)` the IR declines
  (48 pinned functions, four groups, every value right without the writers) stay in the writers,
  closed builds only — an IR spelling that would not speed the interpreter is no reason to move
  a form.  Step 1 (emptying the writers' plan) is not taken.
