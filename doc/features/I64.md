# @I64 — Bytecode compiler (IR -> bytecode)

## What it does

Lowers the parser's tree-shaped IR into a flat stream of bytecode instructions for the interpreter, and initialises the native-function registry. It is the bridge between the structured IR and the linear form the VM executes.

## Where it lives

`src/compile.rs`
